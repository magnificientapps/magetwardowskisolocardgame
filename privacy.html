Privacy Policy — Mage Twardowski Solo Card Game

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Mage Twardowski Solo Card Game is developed by Robert Mikoda.

Data Collection

The game does not require you to provide your name, email address or other contact details to play. It does not send your saved games, preferences or challenge scores to the developer.

The Android version uses Google Play Games Services, which processes certain information as described below.

Local Storage

The game stores preferences, saved games, challenge and campaign scores, gameplay progress and achievement records locally on your device. Optional graphics performance measurements are also stored locally.

This information supports gameplay and preserves your progress between sessions. Saved games and scores are not uploaded to a developer-operated server.

Google Play Games Services

On Android, the game uses Google Play Games Services for achievements. The service starts with the game and may sign you in automatically according to your Play Games settings.

Achievement data sent by the game. When you are signed in, the game sends Google an achievement identifier with a request to mark that achievement as unlocked on your Play Games account. These achievements record milestones such as completing the tutorial, challenges or campaigns, and winning with particular schools of magic, difficulty levels, adversaries, locations or talismans. Achievements earned offline or recovered from existing local progress may be submitted when a connection and authenticated account become available.

Other game data. The game does not upload saved games, screenshots, preferences, numerical challenge or campaign scores, or detailed gameplay logs to Play Games. It does not request your profile name, avatar, email address or friends list from Play Games.

Data handled by Google. Google handles Play Games sign-in and account identity, including your gamer name and avatar, and associates submitted achievements with your account. Its SDK also automatically collects analytics and diagnostic information to improve SDK stability and Google’s products. Google’s Play Games SDK data collection documentation describes these SDK practices and other optional features; this game’s use of Play Games is described above.

Google’s handling of this information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising, Analytics and Data Sharing

The game contains no advertising and uses no separate analytics or tracking services. Google Play Games Services’ data processing is described above.

The developer does not sell or rent user data.

Data Retention and Deletion

Local game data remains on your device until it is removed. You can delete it by clearing the game’s app storage through your device settings.

Clearing local storage does not delete information already stored by Google Play Games. You can manage or delete that information using Google’s controls, as explained in Delete your Play Games data or profile. Google’s retention practices are described in its Privacy Policy.

AI-Generated Assets

This game includes artwork, music and sound effects created using generative AI during development. These assets are included with the game. No AI content is generated during gameplay.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact Robert Mikoda at magnificientapps@gmail.com.

If you contact the developer by email, your email address and the information you provide will be used to respond to your request. You may request deletion of that correspondence using the same address.

Changes to This Policy

Any changes to this privacy policy will be published on this page, and the “Last updated” date will be revised.
