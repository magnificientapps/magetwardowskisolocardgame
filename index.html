Mage Twardowski Solo Card Game

A solo dark fantasy card game inspired by Polish legends. Master magic, face the Devil’s pact, and fight for the sorcerer’s soul.

Master your magic

Choose a school of magic, study the Grimoire to expand your Spellbook, and combine symbols into powerful runes. Manage your Focus and protect your Resolve as you battle Adversaries and their Minions.

  • Learn to play with a guided tutorial.
  • Play a Duel or test your strategy in challenges.
  • Follow a story campaign through a world of magic and folklore.
